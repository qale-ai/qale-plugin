# Privacy

Qale runs on your computer. This page says what it does with your data.

## What Qale stores

- Your memory: markdown files in the memory folder you choose at set-up
  (default `~/Qale/Memory`). Git in that folder keeps the history, so that
  a batch can be undone.
- Your inbox: the transcripts and files you give it, in the inbox folder
  you choose (default `~/Qale/Inbox`).
- Its settings, its queue and its search index, in `~/.qale` (or the
  folder that `QALE_HOME` names).

You can read, move, back up or delete all of these. To delete everything,
delete those folders.

## What Qale sends

Nothing. The Qale server makes no network calls. It sends no telemetry,
and it checks your licence key on your computer.

## What your AI app sees

Qale works inside your AI app (for example Claude Code or Claude Desktop).
The model in that app reads what Qale gives it: transcripts, notes and the
answers to your questions. That data goes to the company that runs the
model, under your agreement with that company, not with us. Use a plan and
settings that fit the data in your meetings.
